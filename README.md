# 🌍 DISASTERX AI

### AI-Powered Natural Disaster Intelligence & Decision Support Platform

> **See the Risk. Understand the Threat. Act Before Disaster.**

DISASTERX AI is a disaster intelligence and decision-support platform that combines **Artificial Intelligence, GIS, satellite imagery, disaster data, IoT monitoring, risk analysis, and emergency alert coordination** in one unified system.

The platform is designed to help users understand **where disaster risk exists, why the risk is increasing, what areas may be affected, and what actions can be taken.**

---

## 🚨 Problem

Natural-disaster information is often scattered across different sources and difficult to understand quickly.

Emergency teams, institutions, and communities need a system that can transform complex disaster information into **clear geographic intelligence and actionable decisions**.

---

## 💡 Solution

DISASTERX AI brings multiple disaster-intelligence capabilities together:

- 🗺️ Interactive GIS disaster maps
- 🌊 Flood-risk analysis
- 🛰️ Satellite imagery
- 🤖 AI-assisted risk explanation
- 📊 Disaster analytics
- 📍 Regional and district-level analysis
- 📅 15-day risk scenario tracking
- 📡 IoT-based monitoring
- 🏫 Venue-level disaster monitoring
- 🏢 3D auditorium digital twin
- 🗺️ Potential impact-zone visualization
- 🚨 Alert preparation and coordination

# ⭐ SPACE EYE

### Assam Flood Intelligence Dashboard

**SPACE EYE** is the dedicated disaster-monitoring module of DISASTERX AI.

The current demonstration focuses on:

📍 **Assam, India**

🌊 **Brahmaputra Basin**

SPACE EYE combines geographic intelligence, satellite imagery, environmental indicators, IoT-style monitoring, risk analysis, and a 3D venue digital twin.
